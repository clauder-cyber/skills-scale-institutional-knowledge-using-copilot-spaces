# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository contains the core project management process documents used by OctoAcme. The documentation is designed to give teams a shared understanding of how projects are initiated, planned, executed, communicated, released, and improved over time.

## Project Management Process Summary

OctoAcme uses a structured lifecycle to move work from idea to delivery. Projects begin with initiation, where the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success metrics, and decides whether the effort is ready to move into planning. Planning then turns the initiative into a prioritized backlog, defines milestones, identifies dependencies, and documents the quality expectations needed for work to be considered done. During execution, the team manages day-to-day delivery through standups, progress tracking, reviews, and blocker escalation, while keeping quality and test coverage integrated into the workflow.

Communication is a core part of the process and is coordinated through regular check-ins, stakeholder updates, and documented escalation paths. Risks and dependencies are tracked in a shared register, and teams are expected to surface blockers early so decisions can be made quickly. When a release is ready, OctoAcme standardizes deployment preparation, smoke testing, rollback planning, and communication so changes are introduced with clear operational awareness. After each milestone or release, the team captures lessons learned and follows up on improvement actions to drive continuous improvement.

## Core Process Areas

- Initiation: validate the business need, stakeholders, goals, and success criteria before starting a project
- Planning: define scope, backlog, milestones, dependencies, and quality gates
- Execution & Tracking: manage work in progress, monitor blockers, and keep delivery aligned with milestones
- Risk & Communication: log risks, escalate issues, and provide regular status updates to stakeholders
- Release & Deployment: prepare for launch, validate production readiness, and communicate releases clearly
- Retrospective & Continuous Improvement: capture lessons learned and track improvements forward

## How to Use These Docs

Use the overview and role docs as a quick onboarding reference, then open the specific process documents when a team needs more operational detail. These process guides are intended to be practical and shareable across cross-functional teams, helping ensure consistent project execution and clearer stakeholder alignment.
